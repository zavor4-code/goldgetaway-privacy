Privacy Policy

Gold Getaway: Thief Puzzle · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Gold Getaway: Thief Puzzle (package name com.zavor4.goldgetaway) handles. The game is published by Mykola Zavorotnyi (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information about you. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: reached levels, coins, boosters, settings (language, sound, music volume, difficulty), whether the tutorial was shown, and the time when the next attempt after a defeat becomes available. This data is never sent to us. You can delete it by clearing the game’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. An ad is shown only when you choose to watch it, for example to start a new attempt right after a defeat.

To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, operating system version, language), and your interaction with ads. Google may use this information to show personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your choices and the laws of your country.

  • Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form (provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. As described above, we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device. Information handled by Google AdMob is governed by Google’s own policies linked above.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For anything Google collects through ads, you can use the controls described above and the tools in your Google account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will also appear in the game’s store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy: zavor4@gmail.com
